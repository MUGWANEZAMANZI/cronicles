The Astronomical Clock of Prague & The Blinded Horologist

Legend insists master Hanuš was blinded with hot iron rods by Prague councillors so he could never replicate the Orloj for another city. Historical municipal ledgers and guild testimonies tell a startlingly different tale of mechanical triumph, court appointments, and guild rivalries.

auto_stories Folklore Myth

Blinded with hot needles by jealous civic leaders; in revenge, he reached into the gears before dying, cursing Prague for 400 years.

fact_check Archival Record (1459)

Jan Růže (Hanuš) served as official city horologist until 1497, survived in good health, and received an honorary municipal pension.

The Golem of Prague & Rabbi Judah Loew

Index #CR-0481

One of the most famous kabbalistic legends claims the Maharal constructed an anthropomorphic clay defender against antisemitic pogroms.

cancel Mythological Lore

A colossal clay automaton animated through divine incantations of the Shem HaMephorash, deactivated and hidden in the attic of Prague's Altneuschul.

check_circle Corroborated History

Rabbi Loew wrote extensively on philosophy and halakha, never mentioning a golem. The legend was authored in 1834 by romantic revivalist Yudl Rosenberg.

The Cataclysmic Fire of the Library of Alexandria

Index #CR-0112

Pop history often mourns a single apocalyptic night when Julius Caesar or Caliph Omar set the entire sum of classical human knowledge ablaze.

cancel Mythological Lore

A singular catastrophic bonfire by Roman legions that wiped out 700,000 unique scientific volumes and set back human discovery by a millennium.

check_circle Corroborated History

Gradual institutional decay, city harbor warehouse burnings in 48 BCE, imperial defunding under Caracalla, and the Serapeum closure in 391 CE.

Nicholas Flamel & The Philosopher's Stone

Index #CR-0925

Celebrated in occult literature as the immortal French clerk who synthesized the Lapis Philosophorum and granted eternal life to himself and Dame Perenelle.

cancel Mythological Lore

Deciphered the mysterious 'Book of Abraham the Jew' on April 25, 1382, transmuting pounds of mercury into pure gold and achieving immortality.

check_circle Corroborated History

A successful manuscript copyist and Parisian landlord who amassed wealth via dowry and real estate. Alchemical treatises bearing his name were 17th-century fabrications.
